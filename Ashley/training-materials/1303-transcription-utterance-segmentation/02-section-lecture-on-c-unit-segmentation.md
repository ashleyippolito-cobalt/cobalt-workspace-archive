Section outline

When you segment the stream of speech into utterances you will use such cues as grammatical structure, intonation, and pausing to determine where one utterance ends and the next begins.

In the following lecture, Joyelle Divall-Rayan, MS, CCC-SLP, discusses c-unit segmentation, providing tips and examples. In this lecture, she refers to the document Segmenting Utterances into C-units which contains detailed descriptions of the segmentation rules.
