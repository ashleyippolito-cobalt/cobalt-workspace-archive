# Section: Determining an Utterance

<!-- https://classes.saltsoftware.com/course/section.php?id=66 -->

Section outline

Determining what constitutes an utterance for speakers with language impairments is not always a straightforward task because intelligibility and/or prosody may be impaired. Utterance segmentation is relatively easy when the speaker produces short, simple utterances preceded and followed by utterances of another speaker. For example,

E What color is this?
C Yellow.
E That/'s right.

Utterance segmentation becomes more complicated when the speaker produces complex utterances or multiple utterances per speaking turn. Although you may define your own rules for segmenting utterances, the samples stored in the SALT reference databases were segmented into communication units.
