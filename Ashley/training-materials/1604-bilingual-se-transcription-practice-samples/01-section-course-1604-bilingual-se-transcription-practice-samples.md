# Section: Course 1604: Bilingual SE - Transcription Practice Samples

<!-- https://classes.saltsoftware.com/course/section.php?id=191 -->

Section outline

Three practice audios are provided which incorporate all the features of Spanish transcription. You can practice your transcription skills by downloading and transcribing the audio samples. You can complete as many transcripts as you wish and compare your transcription with the "master" transcripts.

Materials Needed:

SALT 24 or SALT 20 software. Older versions of the software may be used though you may notice differences in the menu structures and the reports.
A digital playback system for playing the audio while you type in the text.
Adobe Reader® or other PDF reader which is needed to view document files.
