<!-- https://classes.saltsoftware.com/course/section.php?id=156 -->

Section outline

Learn how to assign NSS scores and then compare these scores to those of age-matched peers selected from the Narrative Story Retell reference database.

Work through this exercise where you are given step-by-step directions as you run the software. The transcript used in this exercise is based on the story A Porcupine Named Fluffy. Although you are directed to assign specific NSS scores, the focus of this exercise in on the mechanics of assigning scores and comparing them to database samples. The actual scores you assign are not important.
