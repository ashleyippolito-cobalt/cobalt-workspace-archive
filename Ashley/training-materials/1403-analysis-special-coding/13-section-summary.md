Section outline

Dan continues to demonstrate profound expressive language deficits characterized by echolalia and repeated words. He did, however, improve his therapy goal of increasing appropriate responses to questions. Dan increased the number of appropriate responses from time one to time two, and decreased the number of inappropriate and off-topic responses.

Keep in mind that this is just one illustration of how to apply very specific codes to a language sample to document various aspects of language. You may have other therapy goals which could be documented through a specific coding scheme of your own. Applying specialized coding schemes allows you to quantify and track specific features of the speakers' language. Use the utilities built into SALT to simplify coding. Create a list of your codes and insert them consistently within and across your language samples. Then select from a variety of code reports to count your codes and pull up the words and utterances containing them.
