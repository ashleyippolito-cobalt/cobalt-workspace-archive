# Section: Making a Case for LSA

<!-- https://classes.saltsoftware.com/course/section.php?id=313 -->

Section outline

Speech-Language clinicians are faced with a unique challenge when assessing the productive language of children who are having difficulty acquiring English as a second language. These clinicians are expected to differentiate between children with language disorder and those with poor linguistic skills. The following article, by Raul Rojas and Aquiles Iglesias, makes the case for using language sample analysis with bilingual norms as an important component of the assessment process. It includes two case studies to illustrate the process.

Read the article Making a Case for Language Sampling: Assessment and Intervention With (Spanish/English) Second-Language Learners by Rojas, R. & Iglesias, A., published in The ASHA Leader (2009, March 3). This article won the ASHA Leader Outstanding Contribution Award in the field of Speech-Language Pathology for 2009.
