Section outline

Contractions

 /N'T, /'T	Negative contractions. Examples: can/'t, does/'nt
Do not mark irregular forms (e.g., won't) or when the sound of the root changes (e.g., do --> don't).
Do not slash the word ain't.
 /'S, /'M, /'RE, /'LL, /'D,
/'VE	Contractible verb forms IS, AM, ARE, WILL, WOULD, and HAVE. Examples: he/'s, I/'m, we/'re, we/'ll, we/'d, we/'ve
/H'S,
/H'D,
/DS, /D'D, /'US	Contracted HAS, HAD, DOES, DID, US. Examples: It/h's been too long. He/h'd better look where he/'s go/ing. What/d's he do? Why/d'd the boy look for the frog? Let/'us go.



Use the slash to separate the verb stem from the contraction.
