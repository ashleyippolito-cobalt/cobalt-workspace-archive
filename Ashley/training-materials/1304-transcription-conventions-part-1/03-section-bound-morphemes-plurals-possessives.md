# Section: Bound Morphemes: Plurals & Possessives

<!-- https://classes.saltsoftware.com/course/section.php?id=73 -->

Section outline

Regular plural inflections

Use the bound morpheme /S to indicate regular plural inflections, e.g., frog/s, tree/s. The /S is added to the noun stem without changing the spelling of the stem word, e.g., babies --> baby/s.

The plural bound morpheme is not used for those words which do not have singular forms, e.g., in the woods, tore his pants.

Irregular plural inflections

Do not mark plurals when they are irregular (e.g., geese, deer, mice) or when the sound of the root changes (lives, scarves).

Possessives

Use the bound morpheme /Z to mark possession, e.g., the boy/z hat, a day/z drive

Do not mark possessive pronouns, e.g., mine, his, hers, ours, yours, its, theirs.

Plural possessives

Use the bound morpheme /S/Z to indicate both plurality and possession. The plural inflection comes first followed by the possessive inflection, e.g., the frog/s/z baby/s, two day/s/z drive.
