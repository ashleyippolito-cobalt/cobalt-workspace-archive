Section outline

In this section, you will link samples on your own and answer questions to monitor your understanding.

About the samples. Two samples were collected from Dan, the first when he was 11;2 and then again six months later when Dan was 11;8. Dan is diagnosed with Autism and ADHD and falls on severe end of the Autism Spectrum. He has limited language skills. Dan uses oral language to communicate but has difficulty interacting with peers, responding to questions, and staying on topic. He tends to perseverate on topics and is difficult to redirect back to his work. He has been working on responding appropriately to questions in therapy. In these conversational samples, the traditional SALT elicitation protocol for conversation was not used since Dan is not able to participate effectively in a conversational sample or story retell. This sample focused around a game where dice with pictures on them were used to simulate language. Since response to questions is the area of interest with Dan, a plethora of questions were asked when eliciting his language samples.
