# Section: Skills and Materials

<!-- https://classes.saltsoftware.com/course/section.php?id=46 -->

Section outline

What skills are needed to transcribe the samples?

Transcribers do not need to be experienced clinicians.
Transcribers should be familiar with the elicitation protocol.
Transcribers should be fluent speakers in the language of the sample in order to pick up subtleties in the recordings that non-fluent speakers may miss.
Transcribers should be familiar with the SALT transcript-entry conventions.

Note: You may find it expedient to divide the transcription task into two components: 1) a transcriber, fluent in the language of the sample, glosses the speech; and 2) a transcriber, familiar with the SALT transcript-entry conventions, edits the glossed text into a SALT transcript.


What materials are needed to transcribe the samples?

The SALT editor for typing in the language sample and checking it for transcription errors.
A transcription device for controlled audio or video playback. Consider going digital for better sound quality and more playback options.
Headphones to enhance the audio quality, especially if you will be transcribing in a noisy environment. If you've never worn headphones for an extended period of time, purchase them from a store where you can check them out in person and can return them after a few days if you find that you don't like them.
