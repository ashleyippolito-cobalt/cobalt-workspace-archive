Section outline

Practice transcribing overlapping speech and parenthtical remarks with an excerpt of a conversational sample from Kelina. The audio file and starting SALT transcript file are provided for you. For each sample, you should 1) download the audio file, 2) download the SALT transcript file, 3) code the transcript for bound morphemes, mazes, part words, and unfilled pauses using the SALT editor, headphones, and a digital playback system, and 4) compare your transcript with the "master" transcript.

Sample: Kelina (excerpt from a conversation between Kelina and an examiner)

When coding the sample:

Mark overlapping speech, including examiner feedback.
Mark parenthetical remarks found in the middle of utterances.
Note that, without video, you cannot code for gestures.


After you transcribe the audio sample, view the "master" transcript.
