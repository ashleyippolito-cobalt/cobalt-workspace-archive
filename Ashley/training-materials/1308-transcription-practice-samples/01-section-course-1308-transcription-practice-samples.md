# Section: Course 1308: Transcription - Practice Samples

<!-- https://classes.saltsoftware.com/course/section.php?id=101 -->

Section outline

Eight practice audios are provided which incorporate the various features of standard SALT transcription. You can practice your transcription skills by downloading and transcribing the audio samples.  You may want to complete all transcripts or only a few.  Compare your transcription with the "master" transcript.

Materials Needed:

SALT 24 or SALT 20 software. Older versions of the software may be used though you may notice differences in the menu structures and the reports.
A digital playback system for playing the audio while you type in the text.
Adobe Reader® or other PDF reader which is needed to view document files.
