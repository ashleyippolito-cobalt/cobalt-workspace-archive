# Section: Monolingual Spanish Story Retell Task

<!-- https://classes.saltsoftware.com/course/section.php?id=281 -->

Section outline

The Monolingual Spanish Story Retell database consists of story-retell narratives from native Spanish speaking children. This elicitation protocol uses the wordless picture books:

FWAY: Frog, Where are You (Mercer Mayer, 1969)
FGTD: Frog Goes to Dinner (Mercer Mayer, 1974)
FOHO: Frog on His Own (Mercer Mayer, 1973)
OFTM: One Frog Too Many (Mercer and Marianna Mayer, 1975)


Read the Monolingual Spanish Retell database description and the elicitation protocol.

Elicitation Method

Option 1 (In-person or virtual session): Use SALT's Online Story Elicitation Bilingual Spanish/English & Monolingual Spanish program. Select the category (Monolingual Spanish) and the story (FWAY, FGTD, FOHO, or OFTM). Follow the prompts. There is a YouTube video showing how to elicit a Spanish sample in the Summary section of this course. 

Option 2 (In-person session): Have the speaker seated next to you with the book in front of you. Provide the story model in Spanish while looking at the pictures in the book. There are two ways to present the story model. Both methods should result in similar outcomes (Kim, 2016). Look through the book with the child while you tell a story loosely following the story script or play an audio recording of the story.
