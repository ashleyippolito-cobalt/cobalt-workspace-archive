# Section: Bilingual Story Retell Task

<!-- https://classes.saltsoftware.com/course/section.php?id=215 -->

Section outline

The story retell task requires the student to listen to a story while observing the story's picture sequence, and then retell the same story. This task is repeated for both Spanish and English to provide the opportunity for direct comparison of language proficiency. This narrative task provides the student with optimum support to retell the story in each language - with an audio model provided by the examiner and the visual support of the story book.

The SALT story retell databases contain both English and Spanish samples from over 4,000 English language learners in kindergarten through 3rd grade using the following wordless picture books by Mercer Mayer:

FWAY: Frog, Where are You (1969)
FGTD: Frog Goes to Dinner (1974)
FOHO: Frog on His Own (1973).

Elicitation Method

Option 1 (In-person or virtual session): Use SALT's Online Story Elicitation Bilingual Spanish/English & Monolingual Spanish program. Select the category (Bilingual Spanish/English), the target language (Spanish or English), and the story (FWAY, FGTD, or FOHO). Follow the prompts. There is a YouTube video showing how to elicit a Spanish sample in the Summary section of this course. 

Option 2 (In-person session): Have the speaker seated next to you with the book in front of you. Provide the story model while looking at the pictures in the book. There are two ways to present the story model. Both methods should result in similar outcomes (Kim, 2016). Look through the book with the child while you tell a story loosely following the story script or play an audio recording of the story.

Then give the book to the speaker and ask the speaker to use the book to retell the story in his or her own words. It's amazing how well young children do at this task and how they are able to follow the story and yet use their own vocabulary, sentence structure, and organization to retell the story.
