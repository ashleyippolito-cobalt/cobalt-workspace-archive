# Section: Comprehension Questions

<!-- https://classes.saltsoftware.com/course/section.php?id=323 -->

Section outline

Comprehension questions were developed in English and Spanish for the four frog stories. They are modeled on Bloom's Taxonomy and the questions are specific to the stories. Please note that the samples in the databases were not scored for comprehension.

The full set of comprehension questions are part of the Story Retell Elicitation Materials.
