Part 2: Report Analysis and Interpretation

In this section you will be provided with the Standard Measures Reports of four different speakers. You will not be given any other information about these speakers or their samples. You will be asked questions based solely on each speaker's Standard Measures Report. You may want to print out each report so you have it to refer to when answering the Self Check questions.

Note that these reports were generated using SALT 20 and will vary slightly from the Standard Measures Reports of other versions of the software.
