# Section: Summary

<!-- https://classes.saltsoftware.com/course/section.php?id=126 -->

Section outline

This course demonstrated the process of SALT analysis. A breakdown of the key steps of the analysis process was provided and then put into practice using a case study, directed exercises, and practice exercises. The final set of practice exercises, where you viewed the Standard Measures Reports of four different speakers, showed how SALT can be used to summarize a variety of language patterns. The Performance Report, which provides a narrative of the results, can be edited and copied into your reports. The vast amount of data SALT generates will be invaluable to your clinical work and the Performance Report narrative will save you considerable time when writing your reports.
