# Section: Course 1101: Introduction to LSA

<!-- https://classes.saltsoftware.com/course/section.php?id=14 -->

Section outline

In this introduction to language sample analysis (LSA), begin by listening to Dr. Jon Miller, Professor Emeritus (UW-Madison) and co-founder of SALT Software, as he talks about the role LSA plays in assessing language production. Then get a feel for language sampling by listening to excerpts of language samples collected from speakers of different ages in a variety of contexts. Follow this by watching a short video featuring several school-based clinicians as they talk about how they use LSA and SALT software in their clinical practice. Finally, watch several short case studies which illustrate how LSA can be used effectively to assess language production.

Materials Needed: Adobe Reader® or other PDF reader which is needed to view document files.
