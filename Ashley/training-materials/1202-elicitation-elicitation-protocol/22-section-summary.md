Section outline

Selecting the appropriate sample context is imperative to eliciting a valid and effective language sample. The sampling context you use should be appropriate for the speaker's age and ability, and should be designed to capture the oral language issue(s) in question. If you plan to compare your sample with age or grade-matched samples selected from one of the SALT reference databases, you should follow the database protocol when eliciting your sample. The SALT reference database descriptions and protocols are repeated here for your convenience.
