# Section: Narrative Story Retell Comprehension Questions

<!-- https://classes.saltsoftware.com/course/section.php?id=324 -->

Section outline

Comprehension questions for the four stories included in the SALT Narrative Story Retell database were developed by Mary-Beth Rolland and her colleagues from the Madison Metropolitan School District. They are modeled on Bloom's Taxonomy and are specific to the stories included in the SALT Narrative Story Retell database. Please note that the samples in the database were not scored for comprehension.

The full set of comprehension questions are part of the Story Retell Elicitation Materials.
