# Section: Narrative Story Retell - 2nd & 3rd Grade

<!-- https://classes.saltsoftware.com/course/section.php?id=39 -->

Section outline

This is a story retell task using the following books:

PGHW: Pookins Gets Her Way by Helen Lester (1987) for grade 2, ages 7;0 - 8;11
APNF: A Porcupine Named Fluffy by Helen Lester (1986) for grade 3, ages 7;11 - 9;11



These stories were selected in consultation with the SALT group and clinicians from the Madison Metropolitan School District and the San Diego City Schools. 

Option 1 (in-person or virtual session): Use SALT's Online Story Elicitation  Monolingual English / English Fluent program. Select the PGHW (for 2nd graders) or APNF (for 3rd graders) story and follow the prompts.

Option 2 (in-person session): Use two copies of the book, one with the text covered. Have the speaker seated next to you with the book (text visible) in front of you. Look through the book with the student, while you read the story. Ask the student to use the other book, with the words covered, to retell the story in his or her own words.
