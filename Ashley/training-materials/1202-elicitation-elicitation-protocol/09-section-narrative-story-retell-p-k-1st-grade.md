Section outline

The wordless picture book, Frog, Where Are You? by Mercer Mayer (1969) was used to collect samples from primarily English speakers, ages 4;4 - 7;5. There are a variety of ways to elicit this sample:

Option 1 (In-person or virtual session): Use SALT's Online Story Elicitation Monolingual English / English Fluent program. Select the FWAY story and follow the prompts. 

WATCH this YouTube video showing an in-person elicitation using the Online Story Elicitation program.

Option 2 (In-person session): Have the speaker seated next to you with the book in front of you. Provide the story model while looking at the pictures in the book. There are two ways to present the story model. Both methods should result in similar outcomes (Kim, 2016). Look through the book with the child while you tell a story loosely following a specific script or play the following audio of the story.

Then give the book to the speaker and ask the speaker to use the book to retell the story in his or her own words. It's amazing how well young children do at this task and how they are able to follow the story and yet use their own vocabulary, sentence structure, and organization to retell the story.

While the child will be retelling the story, actively listen by nodding your head, or commenting briefly. Don't be afraid of pauses - be patient and wait.
