Section outline

This is a story retell task using the following book:

DDS: Doctor De Soto by William Steig (1982) for 4th through 6th grade, ages 9;3 - 12;8



This story was selected in consultation with the SALT group and clinicians from the Madison Metropolitan School District and the San Diego City Schools. 

The Protocol

Use two copies of the book, one with the text covered. Have the speaker seated next to you with the book (text visible) in front of you. Look through the book with the student, while you read the story. Ask the student to use the other book, with the words covered, to retell the story in his or her own words.

Key points from video

Make sure student is seated next to you and can easily see the book.
Explain to the student that they will be retelling the story.
Affirm that you are actively listening.
Avoid interrupting the story or asking questions.

IN SUMMARY

Story retell provides consistent content because each student is getting the same story to tell. Story retell allows the analysis of vocabulary and narrative structure. And, because you know exactly what should be included in the story, it allows you to evaluate what's not included. What the student doesn't talk about is often as important as what is included.
