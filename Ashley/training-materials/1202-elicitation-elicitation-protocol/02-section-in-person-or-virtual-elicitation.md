# Section: In-person or Virtual Elicitation

<!-- https://classes.saltsoftware.com/course/section.php?id=329 -->

Section outline

The protocols described in this course cover both in-person and virtual sessions. Most language samples can be elicited in either type of session with similar results (Manning, et al., 2020, Taking Language Samples Home: Feasibility, Reliability, and Validity of Child Language Samples Conducted Remotely with Video Chat Versus In-Person).

Additional articles studying the effects of eliciting language samples virtually: 

Brennan, et al. (2004). The Effect of Videoconference-Based Telerehabiliation on Story Retelling Performance by Brain-Injured Subjects and Its Implications for Remote Speech Therapy
Gaudreau, et al. (2020). Preschoolers Benefit Equally From Video Chat, Pseudo-Contingent Video, and Live Book Reading: Implications for Storytime During the Coronavirus Pandemic and Beyond
Manzanares & Kan (2014). Assessing Children's Language Skills at a Distance: Does it Work?
