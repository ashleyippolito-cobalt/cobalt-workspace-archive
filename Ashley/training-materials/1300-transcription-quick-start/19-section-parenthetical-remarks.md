# Section: Parenthetical Remarks

<!-- https://classes.saltsoftware.com/course/section.php?id=253 -->

Section outline

Parenthetical remarks are captured inside of ((double parentheses)).  Parenthetical remarks are comments that are off-task from the language sample.  A speaker may ask a question during a narrative retell or insert an explanation in the middle of the utterance. Examples:

C The guy ((I forgot his name)) was the robber in the movie.




C The ((what/'s it called)) <> bit him on the nose.

E <Gopher>.




C And those are the rule/s of soccer.

C ((Is that enough))?

E Sure, thanks.
