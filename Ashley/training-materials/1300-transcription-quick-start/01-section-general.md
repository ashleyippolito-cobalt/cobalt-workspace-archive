Course 1300: Transcription Quick Start


Transcription Quick Start provides a summary of the SALT transcription conventions. It is an abridged version to quickly get started with transcription, or to serve as a review for those learners who want to brush up on their transcription skills. 

Materials Needed:

Adobe Reader or other PDF reader which is needed to view document files.
