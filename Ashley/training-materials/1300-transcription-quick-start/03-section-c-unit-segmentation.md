# Section: C-Unit Segmentation

<!-- https://classes.saltsoftware.com/course/section.php?id=245 -->

Section outline

Segmenting utterances into Communication Units (C-units) is the preferred choice for segmentation of language samples. C-units are syntax-based, which allows for greater consistency in utterance segmentation.  The SALT reference database transcripts are segmented using C-units, so if a database comparison is being completed, segmenting utterances into C-units is essential. 

C-Unit Definition:  An independent clause and all its modifiers, including subordinate clauses.

Examples

C I thought the frog jump/ed.	One C-unit with a main and subordinate clause.
C The rule/s are no hitting, no pushing and no face mask/s.	One C-unit with one main clause.
C And then the gnome said, "Becoming a flower is hard."	One C-unit with direct quote and two clauses.


C I went to my friend/z house this weekend.

C And we went to Big_Al's to bowl.

	Two C-units segmented by the coordinating conjunction "and."


C I/'m get/ing a new bike, which will be awesome.

C But I have to wait until my birthday.

	Two C-units. The first C-unit has a main and subordinate clause. The second C unit is segmented at the coordinating conjunction "but."


C You have to go fast so that you don't fall off your board.

	One C-unit with a main and subordinate clause.


C If we don't hurry we/'ll be late.

	One C-unit with a main and subordinate clause. Uses the conditional "if...then" structure with implied "then".

Tips

Look for conjunctions. Coordinating conjunctions, such as "and", "but", "so", and "then", usually signal a new C-unit. Subordinating conjunctions, such as "because", "that", "when", "while", "where", "unless", and "as__as", do not signal a new C-unit and should be included in the utterance.

Look for conditional structures. Clauses using conditional structures such as "if...then" and "either...or" are kept together in the same C-unit.

Segment Fragments.  Sentence fragments are counted as separate C-units if intonation indicates a complete thought has been spoken.

Elliptical Answers.  Elliptical responses, which are sentence fragments in response to a question, are counted as separate C-units. 

Sentence Tags.  When phrases such as "I guess", "you know", or "I mean", are used as tags to an utterance do not segment them into separate C-units.
