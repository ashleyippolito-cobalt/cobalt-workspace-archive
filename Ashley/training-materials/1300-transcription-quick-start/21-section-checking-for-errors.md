Checking for Errors

When you have finished transcribing your sample, it is important to check and fix transcription errors. This ensures the most valid analysis outcome. Click on the red check mark on the top menu bar to check the transcript for errors such as missing end punctuation or unmatched parentheses. SALT does not check for misspelled words.  

If errors are found, SALT opens a dialogue box listing the errors in the transcript. The first error is highlighted. You can edit the transcript for errors. Click the next arrow to move to the next error.
