# Section: Overlapping Speech

<!-- https://classes.saltsoftware.com/course/section.php?id=252 -->

Section outline

Overlapping speech can be an important aspect of pragmatic language skills. Within a transcript overlapping speech is marked with angle brackets < > surrounding the speakers' overlapping speech. Example:

C I went to the store with <my grandma>.

E <Oh, Grandma>!

The overlapping speech convention can also be used if feedback is inserted within a speaker's utterance.  Use empty angle brackets to mark where the inserted words occurred. Example:

C I went to the store < > with my grandma.

E <Uhhuh>.
