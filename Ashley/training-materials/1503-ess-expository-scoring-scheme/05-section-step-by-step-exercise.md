Section outline

Learn how to assign ESS scores and then compare these scores to those of age-matched peers selected from selected from the Expository reference database.

Work through this exercise where you are given step-by-step directions as you run the software. Although you are directed to assign specific ESS scores, the focus of this exercise in on the mechanics of assigning scores and comparing them to database samples. The actual scores you assign are not important.
